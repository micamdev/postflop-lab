# Postflop Lab 1.2.0

**Spots**, and a resizable explorer. If you run 1.1.x, this version installs from inside the
app: click **Update to 1.2.0** in the top bar.

## New

- **Spots:** a spot is a whole preflop situation at the start of the flop: both ranges, the
  pot, the stack, the rake and the line that led there (for example
  `BTN 2.5 · BB 13.5 · BTN call`).
  - A new **Spots** tab in the explorer, next to Ranges and Trees. Spot packs are imported
    with ⋯ › Import JSON…; folders, search, rename, delete and export work as for ranges.
  - **Loading a spot** fills the OOP and IP ranges, the pot, the stack, the rake and the rake
    cap in one go: no more typing the pot of a 3-bet pot by hand.
  - **The line stays in view** at the top of the Setup, with the pot and the stack in big
    blinds. It says "modified" when you change the ranges, the pot, the stack or the rake.
  - **Trees and spots work together:** a tree loaded over a spot brings its bet sizes but
    keeps the spot's pot, stack and rake. A tree with hand-edited lines asks before those
    lines are removed.
  - **Saved solutions remember their spot** and show its line again when you open them.
- **Resizable explorer:** drag its right edge to widen it (double-click it for the default
  width); the width is kept. Long names also show in full on hover, as does the solution's
  name in the top bar.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `.msi` installer, or the standalone `postflop-lab.exe`. Only the `.msi`
  updates from inside the app.
- **Linux:** `.AppImage`, which updates from inside the app, or the `.deb` package.
